# M-Santé Product Requirements Document (MVP)

## Project Overview
M-Santé is a next-generation healthtech "Super App" focused initially on mental health in Senegal, with expansion plans for French-speaking Africa and Europe. It aims to be a trusted platform for patient-practitioner connections, not just a booking app.

## Core Identity
- **Visuals:** Modern, minimalist, "Medical Futurism" (Apple Health + Calm + Tesla UI).
- **Colors:** Light blue, soft white, subtle gold.
- **Values:** Trust, security, serenity, and premium African inclusion.

## User Roles & Key Features

### 1. Patient
- **Discovery:** Search verified practitioners (psychologists, psychiatrists, coaches) with filters for language, price, and availability.
- **Booking:** Seamless scheduling and local payment integration (Wave, Orange Money, CB).
- **Wellness Tools:** Mood tracker, breathing exercises, and emotional journaling.
- **Management:** Appointment history and easy rebooking.

### 2. Practitioner
- **Profile Management:** Professional profile creation and document upload for verification.
- **Practice Tools:** Agenda management, consultation settings, and payment tracking.

### 3. Administrator
- **Governance:** Practitioner validation, user management, and dispute resolution.
- **Insights:** KPI dashboard (conversion rates, revenue, rebooking stats).

## Technical Foundation
- **Frontend:** Responsive Web/Mobile (Flutter/React style).
- **Security:** GDPR-compliant, encrypted data, strong authentication.
- **Payments:** Modular architecture for African local APIs.

## UX/UI Goals
- Immersive onboarding.
- Glassmorphic, intuitive interfaces.
- Fluid animations and premium feel.
- Mobile-first approach.
